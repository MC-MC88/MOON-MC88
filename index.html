Lunar Event Themes

Each theme reflects a rare astronomical event with distinct visual character and scientific significance.

🌕

Supermoon

A full moon at perigee — the Moon's closest orbital approach to Earth. It appears up to 14% larger and 30% brighter than an average full moon. The November 2016 supermoon was the closest full moon since January 1948.

Perigee Full Moon
🩸

Blood Moon

A total lunar eclipse where Earth completely blocks direct sunlight from the Moon. Sunlight filtered through Earth's atmosphere creates the characteristic deep red glow. Totality can last up to 104 minutes.

Lunar Eclipse
🔵

Blue Moon

The second full moon within a single calendar month, occurring roughly every 2.5 years. Despite the name it appears no different in color. The phrase "once in a blue moon" reflects its rarity. Next: May 31, 2026.

Second Full Moon
🌑

Eclipse

A solar eclipse occurs when the Moon passes directly between Earth and the Sun. During totality the Sun's corona — the outer plasma atmosphere — becomes visible to the naked eye. The path of totality is only ~160 km wide.

Solar Eclipse
